This project is a Power BI sales analytics dashboard designed to analyze product sales across multiple distribution channels and evaluate their contribution to total revenue.

The dashboard enables business teams to compare sales volume and revenue performance by channel, helping identify the most effective sales platforms and optimize distribution strategies.

Dashboard Overview:

The report provides an interactive view of sales performance across different sales channels, including:
Dealer
Amazon
WooCommerce
Direct Store

It allows users to explore how each channel contributes to total sales volume and revenue generation.
